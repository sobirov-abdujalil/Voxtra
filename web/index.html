How it works

  1. 1. SpeakSay what you would do, out loud, into your microphone.
  2. 2. Scenario reactsThe simulation applies your decision and answers with a consequence.
  3. 3. Evidence recordedEvery turn is logged with intent, state change, and score.
  4. 4. After-action reportFinish the drill to open your scored, evidence-backed report.

Microphone required. Chromium (Chrome or Edge) recommended — allow microphone permission when prompted. If voice fails, the text intent buttons work the same way.
